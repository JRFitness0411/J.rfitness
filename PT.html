1-1 Personal Training — In-Person Support That Delivers Results

Prefer training face-to-face? My 1-1 personal training gives you dedicated, one-on-one attention to help you hit your goals — with form feedback, real-time motivation, and a plan built around the equipment at your local leisure centre.

Where We Train

I offer 1-1 sessions at three convenient Glasgow locations:

  • Bellahouston Leisure Centre
  • Tollcross Leisure Centre
  • Gorbals Leisure Centre

Let me know which one works best for you, and I’ll share venue details and booking info.

What Makes In-Person Training Different?

  • Real-time form feedback to prevent injury and get better results
  • Personalised attention — every minute of your session is focused on YOU
  • Built-in accountability to keep you consistent
  • Adaptable workouts based on what equipment is available that day
  • Motivation when you need it most — I'll push you to hit your potential

Pricing

1-1 personal training sessions follow the official Glasgow Life PT prices, which are standard across all three leisure centres. When you enquire, I’ll send over the full pricing structure, session length options, and booking details.
