Fitness changed my confidence. Now I help others do the same.

Hi, I'm Jack

My passion for fitness started back in school, where training helped transform my confidence and mindset. When I left school, I knew I wanted to build a career in fitness.

I started by gaining my Level 2 Gym Instructor qualification and went on to spend over three years working in gyms, developing my knowledge, experience and coaching skills. I then took the next step and qualified as a Personal Trainer.

My Approach

I believe training should be built around you. I take the time to understand your goals and create training that suits your experience, lifestyle and ability.

Whether your goal is to build muscle, lose body fat, get stronger or simply feel more confident in the gym, I'll be there to support you, keep you accountable and help you recognise the progress you're making.

Why I Coach

Fitness has given me more than just physical strength. It helped me build confidence, discipline and a better mindset, and that's something I want other people to experience too.

I know walking into a gym or starting a new programme can feel intimidating. My job isn't just to give you exercises — it's to help you understand what you're doing, why you're doing it, and build the confidence to keep progressing.
